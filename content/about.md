I'm Sam Ezebunandu, an engineer based in Calgary, Alberta.

My career started in traditional engineering and oil & gas before moving into software, infrastructure, and security. I currently work around the cloud-native ecosystem and write about the systems and tools I'm learning and building with — especially security engineering, Go, distributed systems, Kubernetes, and CI/CD supply-chain security.

I'm also interested in embedded systems and automation, and I want to spend more time with Rust as a language for that kind of work over time.

This site is my durable home for technical notes, essays, experiments, and longer-form thinking outside rented platforms.
