I'm Sam Ezebunandu, an engineer based in Calgary, Alberta.

I came to software through a non-traditional path. My career started in traditional engineering and the oil & gas industry, where I spent time around physical infrastructure, operational constraints, industrial systems, and the kind of engineering work where mistakes tend to have real-world consequences. That background still shapes how I think about reliability, safety, systems, and trade-offs.

Over time, I moved deeper into software, infrastructure, and security. I currently work around the cloud-native ecosystem and write about the systems and tools I'm learning and building with — especially security engineering, Go, distributed systems, Kubernetes, and CI/CD supply-chain security.

I'm also interested in embedded systems and automation, partly as a continuation of that earlier engineering background. Rust is a language I want to spend more time with for that kind of work over time.

This site is my durable home for technical notes, essays, experiments, and longer-form thinking outside rented platforms.
